A Family Story Rooted in Cantonese Tradition

The Golden Crust began with the Lau family, who arrived in New York from Guangdong with a deep love for Cantonese baking and a desire to build something of their own. They settled in Manhattan’s Chinatown, where the familiar smell of warm egg tarts and flaky pastries reminded them of home and inspired their dream of opening a bakery.

The first generation spent years working in neighborhood bakeries and restaurants, learning the pace of New York while preserving the flavors they grew up with in southern China. Their dedication and craft eventually led them to open a small Cantonese bakery in the early 1990s, a cozy spot where neighbors stopped in for fresh tarts and weekend treats.

As the family grew, so did their vision. The second generation, raised in New York and shaped by the creativity of the city, imagined a bakery that honored their parents’ traditions while introducing new flavors inspired by the city around them. This vision became The Golden Crust.

They brought the bakery to Greenwich Village, where the blend of tree‑lined streets, cafés, and artistic energy offered the perfect home for a new chapter. Here, classic Cantonese techniques meet modern New York creativity, resulting in the mini tarts that define The Golden Crust today.

Every tart reflects the family’s journey — the traditions they carried from Guangdong, the years spent perfecting their craft in Chinatown, and the bold ideas shaped by life in the Village. The Golden Crust is their story, baked fresh each morning.
